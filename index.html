Professional summary

Jayaram is a BBA student at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore with hands-on experience in healthcare distribution, B2B and B2C sales, marketing and business operations. He has worked with hospital procurement teams, healthcare professionals, medical-device manufacturers and individual consumers.

He combines that commercial exposure with interests in finance, business analysis and technology, and is independently developing a gear-driven mechanical compound bow design, with additional experience in electronics prototyping and embedded systems.

Key takeaways

  • The commercial side. Sales and marketing in healthcare distribution since May 2023, from apprentice to executive.
